## Unit Economics

### Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

| Channel | Cost per Install | Conversion to Paid | Effective CAC |
|---------|-----------------|-------------------|---------------|
| Organic (SEO/ASO) | EUR 0 | 8% | EUR 0 |
| Social media ads | EUR 2.50 | 6% | EUR 42 |
| Influencer partnerships | EUR 1.80 | 10% | EUR 18 |
| Vet clinic referral | EUR 5.00 | 15% | EUR 33 |
| Kennel club partnership | EUR 0.50 | 12% | EUR 4 |
| **Blended average** | **EUR 1.90** | **9%** | **EUR 21** |

### Lifetime Value (LTV)

| Segment | ARPU/month | Avg. Retention | LTV |
|---------|-----------|----------------|-----|
| Free user | EUR 0.50 (ad/transaction) | 8 months | EUR 4 |
| Pro pet owner | EUR 4.99 | 14 months | EUR 70 |
| Breeder Essential | EUR 19 | 18 months | EUR 342 |
| Breeder Professional | EUR 49 | 24 months | EUR 1,176 |
| **Blended (paying)** | **EUR 14.50** | **16 months** | **EUR 232** |

### Key Ratios

| Metric | Target | Benchmark |
|--------|--------|-----------|
| LTV/CAC | 11x | >3x is healthy |
| Payback period | 1.5 months | <12 months is good |
| Monthly churn | 5-7% | Industry avg: 8-10% |
| Free-to-paid conversion | 10% | Industry avg: 3-5% |
| Net revenue retention | 110% | >100% is expansion |

## Key Assumptions

1. Sweden pet owner base: 2.5M households with pets
2. Digital adoption rate for pet services: 12% and growing
3. Breeder willingness to pay for verified status: validated by kennel club surveys
4. Average time to convert free user to paid: 45 days
5. Viral coefficient: 1.3 (referral program + social sharing)
6. Seasonality: breeding peaks in spring/fall (2x normal volume)
