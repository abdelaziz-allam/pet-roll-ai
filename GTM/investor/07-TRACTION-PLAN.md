# Traction Plan - Build Proof Before Pitching

## Why Traction Matters

Investors at pre-seed don't expect revenue, but they DO expect signals that the product solves a real problem. Every signal you build increases your valuation and reduces investor risk perception.

**Goal:** Reach these milestones BEFORE your first investor meeting.

---

## 30-Day Traction Sprint

### Week 1-2: Real Users

| Action | Target | How |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Get 50 real users on the app | 50 signups | Share with pet owner communities |
| Swedish dog Facebook groups | 10 posts | Join Hundar i Sverige, Hundrasen groups |
| Instagram pet community | 5 posts | Post with #hundar #hundälskare hashtags |
| Personal network outreach | 20 invites | WhatsApp/email to anyone with pets |
| Swedish breeder forums | 5 posts | Introduce Petfolioo to active breeders |

### Week 2-3: Breeder Validation

| Action | Target | How |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Contact Swedish breeders directly | 10 conversations | Find on SKK registry, email/call |
| Get Letter of Intent signed | 3 LOIs | Template: "I would use Petfolioo for..." |
| Vet clinic introduction | 2 meetings | Walk into clinics in your area |
| Collect testimonial quotes | 5 quotes | Ask beta testers for written feedback |
| Document user sessions | 3 videos | Screen share with user trying the app |

### Week 3-4: Metrics & Polish

| Action | Target | How |
|--------|--------|-----|
| Set up Firebase Analytics | Track DAU/WAU | Add event tracking to key flows |
| Collect NPS score | >30 NPS | Survey beta users (7-question survey) |
| Get App Store reviews | 5+ reviews (4+ stars) | Ask testers to rate on Play Store |
| Create demo video | 2-min professional video | Screen record + voiceover |
| Build metrics dashboard | Shareable link | Notion or Google Data Studio |

## Validation Metrics That Impress Investors

### User Engagement (Most Important at Pre-Seed)

| Metric | Impressive Threshold | Why |
|--------|---------------------|-----|
| Week 1 retention | >40% | Shows product stickiness |
| Daily sessions per user | >2 | Shows daily utility |
| Pets created per user | >1.5 | Shows engagement depth |
| Health records logged/user | >3 | Shows core value delivery |
| Time in app per session | >3 min | Shows meaningful usage |

### Market Validation Signals

| Signal | Impressive Threshold | How to Get |
|--------|---------------------|-----------|
| Waitlist signups | >200 | Landing page email capture |
| Letter of Intent from breeders | 3-5 signed | Direct outreach |
| Vet clinic partnership interest | 2-3 | In-person meetings |
| Social media followers | >500 | Instagram/TikTok pet content |
| Press/media mention | 1-2 | Pitch to Swedish pet magazines |

### Revenue Signals (Not Required, But Powerful)

| Signal | What It Proves |
|--------|---------------|
| 1 paying user | Someone will pay for this |
| Pre-orders/deposits | Demand exists before launch |
| Vet clinic sponsorship | B2B revenue potential |
| Breeder willing to pay EUR 19/mo | Unit economics validated |

## Partnership Traction

### Swedish Kennel Club (SKK) Partnership

**Why it matters:** SKK has 300K+ members and is THE authority on dog breeding in Sweden.

| Step | Action | Timeline |
|------|--------|----------|
| 1 | Research SKK's digital strategy and pain points | Week 1 |
| 2 | Email SKK communications team introducing Petfolioo | Week 2 |
| 3 | Propose pilot: "Digital health passport for registered breeders" | Week 3 |
| 4 | Offer to present at SKK regional meeting | Week 4 |
| 5 | Get written partnership interest (even informal) | Week 5-6 |

**What to offer SKK:**
- Free Petfolioo accounts for their registered breeders
- Digital health record that complements their paper registry
- Data insights on breeding health trends (anonymized)
- Co-branded "SKK Verified" badge in the app

### Veterinary Clinic Partnerships

| Step | Action | Value Proposition |
|------|--------|-------------------|
| 1 | Identify 5 clinics near you | Start local |
| 2 | Walk in with 1-page flyer | "Digital health records for your clients" |
| 3 | Offer: "We'll send pet owners to you for checkups" | Referral value |
| 4 | Get written interest from 2+ clinics | Validation proof |
| 5 | Pilot: QR code in clinic -> download app | Measurable |

## Waitlist Strategy

### Before Full Sweden Launch

Create a "Coming to Sweden" waitlist page:

```
URL: petfolioo.com/sweden
Content:
- "Petfolioo is coming to Sweden - Join the waitlist"
- "Get early access + 3 months free Pro"
- Email capture form
- "Are you a breeder? Get verified first" (separate CTA)
```

**Target:** 200+ waitlist signups before first investor meeting

### Launch Incentives

| Incentive | For | Purpose |
|-----------|-----|---------|
| 3 months free Pro | First 100 users | Drive signups |
| Lifetime free verification | First 20 breeders | Supply-side acquisition |
| "Founding Member" badge | First 50 users | Exclusivity/urgency |
| Refer 3 friends = free Pro | All users | Viral loop |

## What "Good Traction" Looks Like at Pre-Seed

| Level | Metrics | Investor Perception |
|-------|---------|-------------------|
| Minimum viable | 50 users, product works, 1 LOI | "They built something real" |
| Good | 200 users, 40% retention, 3 LOIs | "There's demand" |
| Great | 500 users, 1 paying, SKK interest | "This could be big" |
| Exceptional | 1000 users, EUR 1K MRR, vet partnership | "Take my money" |

**Your realistic target in 6 weeks:** Good level (200 users + LOIs + retention data)
