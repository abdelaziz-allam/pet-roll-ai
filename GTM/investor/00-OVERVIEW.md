# Petfolioo - Investor Readiness Roadmap

## Mission

Secure EUR 500K-1.5M pre-seed/seed funding from European or US investors to launch Petfolioo in Sweden, expand across the Nordics, and prepare for US market entry.

## What is Petfolioo?

Petfolioo is a mobile-first platform for pet owners and breeders that combines:
- Pet health management (vaccinations, vet visits, health records)
- Verified breeding marketplace with smart matching
- Pregnancy tracking and care scheduling
- Real-time chat between matched breeders
- Breeder certification and trust system

## Why Now?

- The European pet care market is valued at EUR 25B+ and growing 7% annually
- Sweden has one of the highest pet ownership rates in Europe (57% of households)
- No dominant digital platform exists for responsible pet breeding in Europe
- Post-COVID pet adoption surge created massive demand for trusted breeder networks
- EU Animal Health Law (2021) creates regulatory tailwind for verified breeding platforms

## Funding Strategy

| Phase | Timeline | Amount | Purpose |
|-------|----------|--------|---------|
| Pre-seed | Q3-Q4 2026 | EUR 300K-500K | Sweden launch, team, first 5K users |
| Seed | Q2-Q3 2027 | EUR 1M-1.5M | Nordic expansion, US pilot |

1. **Phase 1: Foundation** (Weeks 1-4) - Build investor materials
2. **Phase 2: Network** (Weeks 5-8) - Identify and connect with investors
3. **Phase 3: Pitch** (Weeks 9-12) - Present and negotiate
4. **Phase 4: Close** (Weeks 13-16) - Due diligence and term sheet
