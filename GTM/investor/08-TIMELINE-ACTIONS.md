## Success Criteria Per Phase

| Phase | Success = | Failure = |
|-------|-----------|-----------|
| Phase 1 (Foundation) | Company formed, 50+ users, 3 LOIs, deck ready | No users, no validation |
| Phase 2 (Network) | 5+ investor meetings scheduled | 0 meetings after 30 emails |
| Phase 3 (Pitch) | 1-2 investors showing strong interest | All rejections, no follow-ups |
| Phase 4 (Close) | Term sheet signed, funds wired | Can't agree on terms |

### If Phase 3 Fails (All Rejections)

1. Analyze common rejection reasons
2. Build more traction (extend to 6 months)
3. Consider revenue-first approach (bootstrap longer)
4. Try accelerator programs (structured path to investors)
5. Pivot investor targets (try angels if VCs said no)
6. Adjust ask amount (maybe EUR 200K is easier to close)
