# Pitch Deck - Slide-by-Slide Structure

Target: 12-15 slides, clean design, data-driven. Use Figma or Pitch.com.

---

## Slide 1: Title

- Logo + tagline: "The trusted platform for responsible pet breeding"
- Founder name, title
- Contact info
- "Pre-seed Round | EUR 500K"

---

## Slide 2: Problem (The Hook)

**For pet owners:**
- No centralized way to manage pet health records digitally
- Finding verified, responsible breeders is fragmented and risky
- Puppy mills and unethical breeding are widespread (80% of online puppy ads in EU lack health documentation)

**For breeders:**
- No platform to prove legitimacy and reach qualified buyers
- Manual record-keeping leads to lost health histories
- No digital verification system exists

**Key stat:** 1 in 4 puppies purchased online in Europe has undisclosed health issues (EU Commission, 2023)

---

## Slide 3: Solution

**Petfolioo - The responsible breeding platform**

Three pillars:
1. **Health Management** - Digital vaccination records, vet visits, medication tracking
2. **Verified Breeding Marketplace** - ID-verified breeders, health-certified pets, smart matching
3. **Pregnancy & Care Tracking** - Full pregnancy journey, care schedules, milestone tracking

Show: 3 phone mockups demonstrating each pillar

---

## Slide 4: Product Demo

- 30-second embedded video or 4 key screenshots
- Highlight the breeding match flow:
  1. Pet listed with health certifications
  2. Smart match found based on breed, location, health
  3. Verified breeder profiles with trust badges
  4. Chat, agree, track pregnancy

## Slide 5: Market Opportunity

**TAM:** EUR 25B European pet care market
**SAM:** EUR 3.2B pet services + breeding segment (Nordics + DACH + UK)
**SOM:** EUR 45M addressable in Sweden (Year 1-2 target)

- 1.8M dogs in Sweden, 200K+ registered breeders in Nordics
- Average pet owner spends EUR 1,200/year on pet care
- Breeding market is EUR 800M+ in Nordics alone (largely offline)

---

## Slide 6: Business Model

| Revenue Stream | Model | Target Price |
|----------------|-------|-------------|
| Breeder subscription | Monthly SaaS | EUR 19-49/month |
| Premium pet owner features | Freemium | EUR 4.99/month |
| Breeding listing boost | Marketplace fee | EUR 9.99/listing |
| Health certification processing | Transaction fee | EUR 14.99/cert |
| Vet partnership referrals | Revenue share | 10-15% |

**Unit Economics Target:**
- CAC: EUR 15-25
- LTV: EUR 180-360 (12-24 month retention)
- LTV/CAC ratio: 7-15x

## Slide 8: Go-to-Market Strategy

**Phase 1: Sweden (Q3 2026 - Q1 2027)**
- Partner with Svenska Kennelklubben (Swedish Kennel Club)
- Onboard 50 verified breeders in Stockholm/Gothenburg/Malmo
- Target 5,000 pet owner downloads
- Content marketing: Swedish pet influencers + vet partnerships

**Phase 2: Nordics (Q2 2027 - Q4 2027)**
- Expand to Norway, Denmark, Finland
- Localize app and marketing
- Target 25,000 total users

**Phase 3: DACH + UK (2028)**
- Germany, Austria, Switzerland
- UK market (largest pet market in Europe)
- Target 100,000+ users

**Phase 4: US Pilot (2028)**
- Start with 2-3 US states (California, Texas, Florida)
- Adapt for AKC (American Kennel Club) standards

## Slide 9: Competitive Landscape

| Feature | Petfolioo | PawSquad | Breeders.net | Good Dog (US) |
|---------|-----------|----------|--------------|---------------|
| Health tracking | Full | None | None | Basic |
| Verified breeders | Yes (ID + docs) | No | Self-reported | Yes |
| Breeding marketplace | Smart match | No | Listings only | Listings |
| Pregnancy tracking | Full journey | No | No | No |
| Chat | Built-in | No | External | No |
| EU focus | Primary | UK only | Global (thin) | US only |
| Mobile-first | Yes | Yes | No (web) | Web + app |

**Moat:** End-to-end verified breeding ecosystem. Competitors do parts, no one does the full cycle.
