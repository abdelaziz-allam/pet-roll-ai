# Legal Structure & Company Formation

## Recommended Structure

### Primary Entity: Swedish AB (Aktiebolag)

**Why Sweden:**
- Launching in Sweden first, need local presence
- Swedish investors strongly prefer Swedish entities
- Access to Swedish government grants (Vinnova, Almi)
- EU passporting for expansion
- Credible for Nordic investors
- Lower share capital requirement: SEK 25,000 (EUR ~2,200)

### Formation Steps

| Step | Action | Cost | Timeline |
|------|--------|------|----------|
| 1 | Reserve company name "Petfolioo AB" | SEK 1,500 | 1-2 days |
| 2 | Open Swedish bank account (SEB/Nordea/Revolut Business) | Free | 1-2 weeks |
| 3 | Deposit share capital (SEK 25,000) | SEK 25,000 | Same day |
| 4 | Register with Bolagsverket (Swedish Companies Registration) | SEK 2,200 | 1-3 weeks |
| 5 | Get organisationsnummer (org number) | Included | With registration |
| 6 | Register for F-skatt (tax registration) | Free | 1 week |
| 7 | Register for VAT (moms) | Free | 1 week |
| 8 | Set up payroll (if hiring) | Via accounting firm | When hiring |
| **Total** | | **~SEK 30,000 (EUR 2,700)** | **3-4 weeks** |

### Alternative: Estonian e-Residency + OUe

If physical presence in Sweden is not immediate:
- Estonian e-Residency allows remote EU company formation
- Lower admin overhead, fully digital
- BUT: Swedish investors may prefer Swedish AB
- Good backup if you're not physically in Sweden yet

## ESOP (Employee Stock Option Plan)

### Why Set Up Early

- Investors expect an ESOP pool (shows you plan to hire)
- Avoids dilution negotiation later
- Standard: 10-15% total pool, create 3-5% now

### Recommended ESOP Allocation

| Role | Options (% of company) | Vesting |
|------|----------------------|---------|
| Head of Growth | 1.5-2% | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| Senior Engineer | 1-1.5% | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| Community Manager | 0.5% | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| Future CTO (if hired) | 2-3% | 4 years, 1-year cliff |
| Advisory board (2-3 people) | 0.25-0.5% each | 2 years, quarterly vesting |

### Vesting Terms (Standard)

- 4-year vesting period
- 1-year cliff (no shares vest until 12 months)
- Monthly vesting after cliff
- Single-trigger acceleration on acquisition (optional)
- Exercise price = fair market value at grant date

- Class 44: Veterinary services, animal care

### IP Assignment Agreement

Critical document - transfers all code/brand IP from you personally to the company:

```
Key terms:
- All existing code for Petfolioo assigned to Petfolioo AB
- All future work product belongs to company
- All brand assets (logo, name, domain) assigned
- Consideration: shares in the company
- Representations: no third-party IP claims
```

**Action:** Have a Swedish startup lawyer draft this (cost: SEK 5,000-10,000)

## Investor-Friendly Terms

### What Investors Typically Want (Pre-Seed)

| Term | Standard | Negotiable? |
|------|----------|-------------|
| Equity stake | 12-20% | Yes |
| Board seat | 1 seat (observer often) | Yes |
| Pro-rata rights | Yes (follow-on investment) | Standard |
| Information rights | Quarterly updates | Standard |
| Anti-dilution | Broad-based weighted average | Standard |
| Liquidation preference | 1x non-participating | Push back on >1x |
| Vesting for founders | 4 years reverse vesting | Negotiate start date |
| Drag-along rights | Yes | Standard |
| Right of first refusal | On secondary sales | Standard |

### SAFE vs. Priced Round

| Instrument | When to Use | Pros | Cons |
|-----------|-------------|------|------|
| **SAFE (recommended for pre-seed)** | Small amounts, quick close | Fast, cheap, standard | Valuation deferred |
| Priced round | Larger amounts, lead investor | Clear valuation, clean | Expensive legal, slow |
| Convertible note | Some European investors prefer | Familiar to all | Debt instrument, interest |

**Recommendation:** Use SAFE notes for angel/small checks, priced round if a VC leads with EUR 300K+.
