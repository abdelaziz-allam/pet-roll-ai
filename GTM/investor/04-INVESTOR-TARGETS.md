# Target Investors & Contact Strategy

## Investor Selection Criteria

We target investors who have:
- Invested in pet-tech, marketplace, or health-tech startups
- Focus on Nordics/Europe or early-stage global
- Check size: EUR 100K-500K for pre-seed
- Value-add: network in pet industry, Nordic market access, marketplace expertise

Would love 15 minutes to show you the product and share our Sweden 
go-to-market plan.

Best,
[Name]

## What Investors Will Ask (Prepare Answers)

1. "Why are you the right person to build this?" - Technical founder, product already built
2. "Why Sweden?" - Highest pet insurance rate, strong breeding culture, digital-mature
3. "What's stopping Good Dog from coming to Europe?" - Regulatory complexity, localization, first-mover in EU
4. "How do you verify breeders?" - ID documents + kennel registration + health certs + admin review
5. "What's your unfair advantage?" - Full product built, EU Animal Health Law compliance, first-mover
6. "How will you compete with classifieds (Blocket)?" - Trust layer + health records + verification (classifieds can't replicate ecosystem)
7. "What are your key metrics?" - [Prepare current numbers: users, retention, engagement]
8. "When do you break even?" - ~4,000 paying users (Month 18-24)
9. "What's the exit?" - Acquisition by pet industry player (Mars/Royal Canin/Purina) or IPO at scale
10. "How do you handle the chicken-and-egg marketplace problem?" - Supply-first: onboard breeders with free health tools, demand follows
