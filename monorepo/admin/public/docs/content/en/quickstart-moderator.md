# Quick Start: Moderator

Welcome to the Petfolioo Admin Portal. As a Moderator, your focus is content quality and community safety. You review verifications, moderate listings, and ensure pet data is accurate. This guide covers your access, daily workflow, and boundaries.

Pages **not** in your sidebar: Analytics, Admin Users, Settings.

## First Steps After Being Granted Access

1. **Log in and check your profile** - Click your avatar to confirm your details are correct.
2. **Open the Verification queue** - This is your primary workspace. See what is pending review.
3. **Browse the Mating page** - Familiarize yourself with active listings and the moderation tools available.
4. **Explore Pets** - Click into a few pet profiles to understand the data structure and what fields you can edit.
5. **Review the Dashboard** - Get a baseline understanding of platform activity levels.

---

## Daily Workflow Checklist

- [ ] Check the Dashboard for a quick health overview
- [ ] Process the Verification queue: review documents, approve legitimate breeders, reject incomplete or fraudulent submissions with clear reasons
- [ ] Moderate Mating listings: flag or remove inappropriate content, verify listing accuracy
- [ ] Review Pets for reported or flagged profiles and correct inaccurate data
- [ ] Check Health Certs for pending certification reviews
- [ ] Ban problematic users who violate community guidelines
- [ ] Scan Feedback for user reports about content issues (even though you cannot respond, you can act on what you read)

---

## Key Responsibilities

### Verification Reviews
Your most important task. Breeders submit documentation to prove their credentials. You review these documents for authenticity, completeness, and compliance with platform standards. Approve legitimate applications promptly and reject fraudulent ones with a clear explanation.

### Mating Moderation
Monitor mating marketplace listings for inappropriate content, misleading descriptions, or policy violations. Use the Moderate action to flag or hide problematic listings.

### Pet Data Quality
You can edit pet profiles to correct inaccurate breed information, fix typos, update health status, or remove inappropriate photos. Keep the pet registry accurate and trustworthy.

### Community Safety
You can ban users who violate platform guidelines. Use this for spam accounts, abusive behavior, or users who repeatedly submit fraudulent verifications.

### Health Certification Review
Review submitted health certifications for accuracy and approve or reject them based on the documentation provided.

## When to Escalate

Contact an Admin or Super Admin when you need to:

- Delete a pet profile that should be removed entirely
- Remove a mating listing (you can moderate/flag, but not delete)
- Respond to user feedback that requires an official reply
- Send a notification to affected users about a moderation action
- Create or edit a blog post about community guidelines
- Access analytics data for a moderation report

---

## Tips for Effective Moderation

- **Be consistent** - Apply the same standards to all verification reviews. Document your reasoning in the reject message.
- **Act promptly** - Verification applicants are waiting. Try to process submissions within 24 hours.
- **When in doubt, escalate** - If a verification case is ambiguous, flag it and ask an Admin for a second opinion rather than making a questionable approval.
- **Ban with care** - Banning is a serious action. Reserve it for clear policy violations, not minor issues.
