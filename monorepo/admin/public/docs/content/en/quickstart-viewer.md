# Quick Start: Viewer

Welcome to the Petfolioo Admin Portal. As a Viewer, you have read-only access to most platform pages. Your role provides visibility into platform operations without the ability to modify any data. This guide covers what you can see, how to use the portal effectively, and how to request elevated access if needed.

---

## Pages Available to You

| Page | Access Level |
|------|--------------|
| Dashboard | View KPIs and analytics charts |
| App Users | View user list and profiles |
| Pets | View pet registry and profiles |
| Pet Categories | View category taxonomy |
| Verification | View queue and submission status |
| Mating | View marketplace listings |
| Health Certs | View certification records |
| Vax Analytics | View vaccination data |
| Feedback | View user submissions |
| Blog | View published posts |
| Notifications | View sent notifications |
| Analytics | View charts and metrics |

Pages **not** in your sidebar: Admin Users, Settings.

---

## What "View Only" Means Practically

Your interface is streamlined compared to other roles:

| UI Element | What You See |
|------------|--------------|
| Create/Add buttons | Hidden - not visible anywhere |
| Edit buttons | Hidden - table rows are not clickable for editing |
| Delete options | Hidden - no action menus with destructive options |
| Approve/Reject buttons | Hidden - verification status shown as read-only text |
| Send Notification button | Hidden |
| Export buttons | Hidden |
| Ban/Unban buttons | Hidden |

You see the same data as other roles, but the interface removes all action controls. This means you cannot accidentally modify anything. Tables, charts, detail views, and status indicators all display normally.

## First Steps After Being Granted Access

1. **Log in and verify your profile** - Click your avatar to confirm your account details.
2. **Explore the Dashboard** - This is your home base. Study the KPI cards and analytics charts to understand platform health at a glance.
3. **Visit Analytics** - Review engagement trends, growth metrics, and platform usage patterns.
4. **Browse App Users and Pets** - Familiarize yourself with the data available for your reporting needs.
5. **Check Verification** - Understand the queue status and approval rate to gauge operational load.

## Key Responsibilities

### Oversight
Your primary value is informed oversight. You can see the full picture of platform operations without needing to take action. Use this to identify trends, spot potential issues early, and keep stakeholders informed.

### Reporting
While you cannot export data directly from the portal, you can use what you see to prepare reports externally. The Dashboard and Analytics pages provide the metrics most commonly needed for stakeholder updates.

### Monitoring
Watch for unusual patterns: sudden drops in verification approvals, spikes in user bans, or declining engagement metrics. When you spot something concerning, raise it with an Admin or Super Admin who can investigate and act.

### Stakeholder Visibility
If you are a business stakeholder, investor, or partner, the Viewer role gives you transparency into platform operations without requiring the team to prepare separate reports for you.

---

## Preparing Reports from View Access

Since you cannot export data directly, here are effective approaches:

1. **Screenshots** - Capture Dashboard and Analytics charts for presentations.
2. **Manual notes** - Record key metrics from KPI cards during your daily review.
3. **Request exports** - Ask an Admin or Super Admin to export data you need for deeper analysis.
4. **Scheduled reviews** - Set a recurring time to review the portal and note changes week over week.

---

## How to Request Elevated Access

If your responsibilities change and you need the ability to take actions (approve verifications, moderate content, manage users), follow this process:

1. Identify which specific actions you need and why.
2. Contact your organization's Super Admin with your request.
3. Specify whether you need a role upgrade (to Moderator or Admin) or just specific additional permissions.
4. The Super Admin can customize your permissions without changing your role label if only a few extra actions are needed.
