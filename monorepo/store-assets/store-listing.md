## Full Description (max 4000 chars)
Petfolioo is the all-in-one pet health management app designed for pet owners and professional breeders. Keep your furry family members healthy, organized, and happy with powerful yet easy-to-use tools.

KEY FEATURES:

Pet Profiles
Create detailed profiles for each of your pets with breed info, photos, weight tracking, and more. Support for dogs, cats, and other companion animals.

Health Records & Vaccination Tracker
Never miss a vaccination again. Log vet visits, track medications, record surgeries, and get smart reminders for upcoming appointments. Generate PDF health reports for travel or breed certification.

Pregnancy Monitor
Track pregnancy milestones week by week, monitor weight changes, and prepare for delivery day with our comprehensive pregnancy tracking tools.

Mating Marketplace
Find verified breeding partners with health certifications and lineage info. Browse listings, send mating requests, and connect with breeders in your area. Smart matching suggests compatible pets based on breed and location.

Breeder Verification
Get verified as a trusted breeder. Our verification system helps build trust in the community and ensures responsible breeding practices.

Smart Reminders & Notifications
Set up automated reminders for feeding, medications, grooming, exercise, and vet visits. Customize notification timing to fit your schedule.

WHY PET OWNERS LOVE PETFOLIOO:

- Free to use with no hidden fees
- Beautiful, intuitive interface
- Secure cloud storage for all records
- Works offline for quick access
- Multi-pet support
- Firebase-secured authentication
- GDPR compliant

PERFECT FOR:
- Pet owners who want organized health records
- Professional breeders managing multiple animals
- New pet parents learning the ropes
- Anyone who wants the best care for their furry friends
